Abstract

Prediction markets are coordination engines. They transform uncertainty into prices, reveal collective conviction, and create transparent incentives around future events. Sky Predict applies this model through a clean SkyUSD market interface while keeping execution, settlement, and intelligence workflows aligned with Ritual Chain.

The protocol centers on SkyUSD-denominated markets for crypto prices and sports outcomes, with a structure that can extend into macro, esports, AI benchmarks, and on-chain event categories without changing the core user experience.

Sky Predict is designed as a Ritual-native forecasting system: on-chain markets provide transparent custody and payouts, while Ritual compute primitives handle the backend path for data retrieval, automation, and AI-assisted market intelligence.

Ritual Stack

Sky Predict is built around Ritual Chain's TEE-EOVMT model: deterministic EVM execution handles token accounting, market state, and payout logic, while delegated Ritual compute supports external data, model inference, and automated market operations behind the interface.

HTTP 0x0801

Provides the backend data path for TEE-attested retrieval of exchange prices, fixture data, and settlement evidence.

JQ 0x0803

Parses JSON responses into typed values that can be mapped into deterministic market outcomes.

LLM 0x0802

Powers protocol-side summaries, rule explanations, research context, and settlement commentary.

Scheduler

Coordinates recurring backend operations such as creation windows, expiry checks, settlement triggers, and agent wakeups.

SkyUSD

SkyUSD is the accounting and participation unit for Sky Predict markets. It provides a consistent denomination across market categories, reduces UI complexity, and creates a stable interface for prediction positions, portfolio accounting, and leaderboard analytics.

  • Used as the primary betting unit across the market interface.
  • Designed for simple portfolio accounting and clear position sizing.
  • Supports streamlined testnet distribution for early users and protocol testing.
  • Provides a consistent base for analytics, rewards, and reputation systems.

Architecture

The Sky Predict architecture separates market custody, market deployment, backend settlement workflows, and user experience. This modular approach keeps the visible interface simple while allowing Ritual-native compute to operate as protocol infrastructure.

Market contracts

Hold pools, track user positions, enforce deadlines, store resolution state, and distribute payouts.

Factory layer

Creates standardized markets and exposes a discoverable registry for the frontend and operations layer.

Settlement flow

Uses receipt-aware updates, clear market state, and deterministic payout rules for user-facing reliability.

Ritual compute layer

Connects HTTP, JQ, LLM, Scheduler, AsyncJobTracker, RitualWallet, and TEE executor workflows beneath the market UI.

AI-Assisted Market Intelligence

Sky Predict uses Ritual's AI-native architecture as an intelligence and operations layer around the core market system. The primary market experience remains simple: users choose a market, place a SkyUSD position, and claim after resolution. AI components run as protocol-side support for market description, monitoring, risk context, and settlement explanation.

  • Market intelligence modules organize event context, historical movement, and category-specific risk signals.
  • Research summaries help users understand market background without replacing their own decision-making.
  • Resolution support tools structure settlement evidence and make final outcomes easier to audit.
  • Natural-language interfaces can explain rules, exposure, and settlement logic in a clear user-facing format.

Resolution Layer

Reliable market resolution requires three properties: clear rules, dependable data, and transparent execution. Sky Predict's resolution design keeps the user-facing flow simple while Ritual-native workflows handle data retrieval, parsing, scheduling, and settlement evidence behind the scenes.

  1. A market defines its accepted rules, data context, and outcome mapping before betting opens.
  2. Users place SkyUSD positions through the visible market interface before the deadline.
  3. Backend resolution workflows collect and normalize category-specific settlement data.
  4. Ritual-compatible parsing maps the final value to the winning outcome through deterministic rules.
  5. The market records the final outcome and unlocks winner claims.

Security and Trust Model

Sky Predict's security model prioritizes transparent custody, explicit deadlines, visible market rules, and minimized manual operational trust. TEE-backed delegated compute, async lifecycle tracking, callback validation, and scheduled workflows form the protocol-side safety model without requiring additional complexity in the user interface.

  • Market custody and payout accounting remain on-chain and auditable.
  • External data workflows are source-bound, reproducible, and evidence-oriented.
  • Async workflows account for retries, expiry, callback validation, and state changes between request and delivery.
  • Production deployments are designed around independent contract audits and operational monitoring.

Expansion Roadmap

The current Sky Predict market stack is established around contracts, SkyUSD, transparent market flows, and Ritual-native backend infrastructure. The roadmap focuses on advanced agentic capabilities that can expand the protocol without cluttering the base market interface.

  • Persistent Agent Network: long-running market research agents with soul, memory, StorageRef persistence, heartbeat monitoring, and CID-based revival.
  • Sovereign Agent Executors: Scheduler-driven agents that wake themselves, run CLI harnesses inside TEEs, analyze markets, and write results back through callbacks.
  • DKMS-protected Strategy Memory: encrypted agent state, private research notes, and protected API credentials using Ritual secret and key-management primitives.
  • Streaming AI Analyst: EIP-712 verified LLM streaming for live market explanations, portfolio summaries, and settlement commentary.
  • Multimodal Markets: event categories that use image, media, and document interpretation for richer real-world prediction markets.
  • Reputation Intelligence: agent-assisted scoring for user accuracy, liquidity quality, dispute behavior, and long-term forecasting reputation.
